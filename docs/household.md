# House rules

This site exists to help our household invest calmly and with evidence. It is a research notebook, not advice.
These rules decide when, and how much, we act on anything it shows.

## Core first
- Most long-term savings belong in a broad, low-fee index fund. Check that the fund actually tracks its index and that
  fees are low (S-017). Buying at all-time highs is normal for an index; highs cluster (S-007).
- This system only ever informs a small **satellite** slice next to that core.
- Proposed limits (owner sets): satellite at most **10%** of investable savings, and any single stock at most **2%**.

## The trust ladder
We act only as far as the record allows. The site computes the current stage from scored picks, using `trust` in settings.
1. **Observe**: the brain is not making real calls yet. Read, learn, act on nothing.
2. **Paper**: real calls are made and scored, but no money moves. This lasts until enough picks are scored and both the
   hit rate and the average excess return over the benchmark clear their thresholds.
3. **Small money**: small satellite positions within the limits above.
4. **Trusted**: a longer record, and high-conviction calls have proven better than low-conviction ones (calibration).

If the numbers fall back below a threshold, we step back down.

## Before acting on any call
1. The call comes from the brain, not from a video, a tip or a headline. Dated claims are unverified.
2. At least 3 of the 4 setup checks pass on the stock page.
3. No earnings report or major macro event falls early in the horizon (S-005, S-013).
4. Write down the "wrong if" condition before buying. If it happens, sell. No averaging down by default.
5. Stay within the size limits. One idea never decides the household's outcome.

## Never
- Never buy only because a stock is at a high, or only because it has fallen a lot.
- Never chase a stock that is stretched far above its trend (S-010, S-019).
- Never put personal holdings, account details or amounts in this repo. It is public.

## Review
- Monthly: read the track record and the calibration. If conviction is not informative, ignore conviction.
- Change these rules only through a pull request with a change note, like every other doc.
